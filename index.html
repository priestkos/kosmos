K'os Mos

A Foxy Fiery Kitsune

Balmung · Crystal  ⟡  Tonberry · Elemental

Profile

Race: Kitsune

Class: Bard, Courtesan and a Mercenary for Hire, whose performances permeate the battlefield.

Homes: The streets and taverns of Balmung, and the quieter alleys of Tonberry.

Bio: A storyteller first and a markswoman second, K'os Mos travels between data centers collecting tales worth setting to melody. You may hear her song upon the battlefield and be assured her bow is never far behind.

Workplaces: The Succubus Den on Materia, OCE and The Velvet Temptress on Dynamis, NA.
